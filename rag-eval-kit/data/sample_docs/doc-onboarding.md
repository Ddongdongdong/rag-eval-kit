# New Customer Onboarding

## Account setup
Onboarding starts the day the contract is signed. Create the customer account within 1 working day, invite the admin user, and confirm the login works before the kickoff call.

## Kickoff and training
Hold a kickoff call in the first 3 working days. The call covers goals, the main admin, and the first project. Offer one training session of 60 minutes in the first 14 days, and share the recording afterwards.

## First 14 days checklist
Day 1: account ready. Day 3: kickoff done. Day 7: first project created. Day 14: check-in call to review usage and answer questions. If the customer has not logged in by day 7, send a help message and offer a short call.

## Success criteria
Onboarding is complete when the customer has created a project, invited at least one teammate, and confirmed they can complete their main task without help.
