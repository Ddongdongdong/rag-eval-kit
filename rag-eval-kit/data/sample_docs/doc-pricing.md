# Pricing and Discount Rules

## Plans
There are three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Starter is for small teams, Growth adds team reporting and priority support, and Enterprise adds custom limits and a dedicated manager.

## Discount approval
A discount of up to 10 percent can be approved by the sales lead. A discount between 10 and 20 percent needs manager approval. No discount above 20 percent is allowed without director approval, and the reason must be written in the CRM.

## Annual billing
Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount compared with monthly billing. The annual price is charged once at the start of the year. Switching from monthly to annual in the middle of a term applies from the next renewal date.

## Price changes
Prices are reviewed once a year. Existing customers keep their current price for 12 months after any increase is announced.
