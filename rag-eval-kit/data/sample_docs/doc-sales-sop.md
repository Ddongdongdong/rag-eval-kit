# Sales SOP — Standard Sales Process

## First contact
When a new lead arrives, reply within 4 working hours. Confirm the customer's name, company, and the problem they want to solve. Never pitch a price before you understand the need.

## Discovery
Ask three discovery questions: what is the current workflow, what is broken or slow, and what does success look like in 30 days. Record the answers in the CRM under the lead record.

## Demo
Book a demo within 3 working days of first contact. The demo should last 30 minutes and show only the three features the customer asked about. Do not show the full feature list.

## Proposal and close
Send a written proposal within 2 working days after the demo. The proposal lists the plan, the price, and the start date. If the customer does not reply in 48 hours, send one follow-up message and one phone call. Do not discount without manager approval.
