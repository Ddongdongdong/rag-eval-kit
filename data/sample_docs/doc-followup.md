# Customer Follow-up Playbook

## Follow-up cadence
Follow up on day 1, day 3, day 7, and day 14 after the first contact. Stop after four touches if there is no reply, and mark the lead as cold in the CRM.

## What to say
Day 1: thank the customer and repeat the next step. Day 3: share one useful example or case. Day 7: ask if the timing has changed. Day 14: close the loop politely and offer to reconnect later.

## Tone and rules
Keep every message short, under 80 words. Never send more than one message per day. Do not use pressure language such as "last chance" unless a real deadline exists. Record every touch in the CRM with the date and the outcome.
