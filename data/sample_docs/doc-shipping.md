# Shipping and Delivery Guide

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 working days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 working days and costs extra. Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same working day.

## Tracking
Every order has a tracking number sent by email after dispatch. Customers can check the tracking link at any time. If tracking shows no movement for 3 days, support should contact the carrier.

## Delivery problems
If a parcel is lost, support opens a carrier claim after 7 working days for standard shipping and after 3 working days for express shipping. If a parcel arrives damaged, the customer should report it within 7 days with a photo, and a replacement is sent free of charge.
