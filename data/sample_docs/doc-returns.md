# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Customers can return a product within 30 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Items marked final sale cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
The customer contacts support with the order number. Support checks the order, confirms the item is inside the 30-day window, and issues a return label by email.

## Refund method and timeline
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once the warehouse receives the returned item, the refund is processed within 5 working days. The bank may need 3 to 7 extra days to show the refund.

## Damaged items
If the item arrived damaged, the customer should send a photo within 7 days. Damaged items are replaced free of charge, and no return shipping fee is charged.
